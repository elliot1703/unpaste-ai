## Dominic's Current AI Workflow

### What he does:
1. **Drafts a letter** → puts it through ChatGPT
2. **Asks it to peruse the letter**, check authorities, find missed references
3. **Gets 1-2 additional references** that he might use
4. **Checks references manually** — goes to primary sources to verify
5. **Document analysis** — gives it a document, asks "analyze this, tell me what page and paragraph you're referring to" → gets a digest
6. **Timeline extraction** — "timeline is fantastic"
7. **Valuation analysis** — put a valuation report through ChatGPT, "really analyzed the valuation very well"
8. **Script review** — uses a hypnotherapy expert persona to review NLP scripts

### How he builds "experts":
- Creates custom personas with specific attributes
- Has a business valuation expert, a hypnotherapy expert, etc.
- Extracts attributes from textbooks and plugs them in
- Tells the AI to find additional attributes it needs and "they update themselves"
- Spends about half a day setting up each expert
- **These experts are saved and reused**

### Privacy workaround:
- Strips all identifying info before using ChatGPT
- Changes "Mr. Smith" to "Mr. Hay", "Mr. Brown" etc.
- Never puts real client names in
- Uses ChatGPT for the analysis, then applies results back to real documents
